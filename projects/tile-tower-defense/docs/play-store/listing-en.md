# English listing draft

## App name

Tile Tower Defense

## Short description

Build island defenses, draft new land, and send airships against rival captains.

## Full description

Build your defenses on a floating island. Choose new land, place towers beside
winding paths, and upgrade their skills as enemy waves approach.

Play against other captains or computer opponents in a shared world. Spend your
resources on defenses or recruit soldiers to send by airship to rival islands.
Your decisions shape each match: expand your island, improve a tower, or launch
an attack before the next wave arrives.

- Draft discoveries to expand your island.
- Build and specialize towers.
- Choose recruits and send airship raids.
- Explore Sky Islands, Volcano, Deep Ocean and Highlands.
- Play with friends or computer-controlled captains.
- Pan and pinch to explore the battlefield on mobile.

Internet connection required. You can play as a guest or sign in with Google or
Discord. Shared matches continue while you are away.
