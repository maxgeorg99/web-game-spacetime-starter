Your island. Your defenses. Their problem.

A quiet patch of land in the clouds. A winding road to your keep. And three other captains with plans of their own.

Tiled Tower Defense is a browser strategy game about building a defense worth keeping—and deciding when to take the fight to another island. Defend against enemy waves, expand your land, and send soldiers across the sky by airship.

Free to play in your browser · Up to 4 captains per world · In development

Build small. Think ahead.

Place towers where they can make every shot count. Mix quick arrows, heavy catapult stones, and long-range fire, then invest in skill upgrades to shape your defense.

Every victory makes room for more.

Survive a wave, earn resources, and choose a new tile for your island. Fit your next expansion around the defense you already have.

The next threat might be your neighbor.

Recruit soldiers, choose a rival island, and launch an airship. Spend on a stronger defense—or send a raiding party while the other captain is busy holding the line.

Inside the current build

  • Four islands, four captains. Share a world with friends or join an existing session.
  • Three tower types. Build and upgrade a defense around your island’s road.
  • Eight recruitable soldiers. Choose your raiding party and its destination.
  • Five enemy waves. Hold your keep, collect rewards, and expand between battles.
  • Saved online worlds. Return to your island and continue building.

Take command

  1. Press Run game, choose a captain name, and join or create a world.
  2. Choose a tower, then select an empty meadow to build. Select a placed tower to upgrade it.
  3. Start the next wave when you are ready. After a victory, choose and place a new land tile.
  4. Open Recruit, choose an opponent, and send soldiers by airship.

Mouse: click to select, drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Arrow keys also move the camera.
Touch: tap to select, drag to pan, pinch to zoom. Fullscreen landscape is recommended.

An internet connection is required. A full world can be spectated.
